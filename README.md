## Mission
Our mission is to create a fun, competitive, and distinctly UBC-themed experience that gives students a quick way to relax, challenge themselves, and connect through shared campus culture.
## Problem
UBC students often have short periods of free time between classes, labs, and other academic commitments, but these breaks are not always long enough for more involved activities. At the same time, university life can be stressful, and students benefit from quick, low-commitment ways to relax and engage with others.
UBC also has a strong campus culture built around shared experiences, faculty stereotypes, course-related jokes, and friendly competition between students. However, there are relatively few lightweight UBC-specific interactive experiences that combine these aspects of campus culture in a way that students can enjoy during short breaks.
This creates an opportunity for a casual activity that is quick to access, easy to understand, and engaging enough to encourage repeated play and friendly competition among UBC students.
